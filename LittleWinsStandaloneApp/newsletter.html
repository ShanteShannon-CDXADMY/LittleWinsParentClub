How do I make family routines feel less overwhelming?

Start with one repeatable moment instead of a full-day overhaul. Choose something that already happens—breakfast, arriving home, or brushing teeth—and add one small step that fits your family.

Try: “After breakfast, we check what we need for today.” Younger children may need hands-on support; older children can help choose the step.

Your encouragement: One steady moment counts. You can build the rest another day.
