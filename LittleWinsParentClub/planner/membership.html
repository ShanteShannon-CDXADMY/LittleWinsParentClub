Little Moves, Real Life

Optional Parent Recharge + Fitness: $9.99/month total. Parent Recharge without fitness is $7.99/month.

Short at-home options for busy moms. Choose a focus, then try the no-equipment or light-equipment example at your own pace.

A Flexible Week: Monday: legs/glutes. Tuesday: a comfortable walk or gentle cardio. Wednesday: upper body. Thursday: mobility or rest. Friday: full body/core. Weekend: optional movement or rest. Skip days and shorten sessions as needed.

Preview only: choices and progress are not saved. The fitness library and workout planning are part of the optional Parent Recharge + Fitness upgrade when subscriptions become available. Use a stable support, comfortable range, and space away from children’s play. Keep weights out of reach. Stop for pain, dizziness, or unusual breathlessness. If pregnant, recently postpartum, injured, or unsure about exercise, ask your clinician about suitable activity.
