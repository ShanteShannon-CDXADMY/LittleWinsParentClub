Neurodivergent children & mental health

Understanding, practical support, and trusted resources for children who think, communicate, and experience the world in different ways. Mental health matters alongside everyday needs.

Neurodivergence and mental health conditions are not interchangeable. Each child has individual strengths and support needs.

What reminders work for a child with ADHD?

Get clarity about screening and evaluation while keeping your child’s strengths in view.

Autism

How can I support my autistic child’s communication?

Make room for words, gestures, pictures, and the communication your child already uses.

Where can I find autism and early-intervention resources?

A short resource path for families in the United States.

Autism

How can I explain a change of plan to my autistic child?

A change may be easier to process when it is concrete, honest, and presented in a preferred communication format.

Autism

How do I make space for a sensory break?

A break can be an access need rather than a reward.

How can I connect through my autistic child’s interests?

A loved interest can offer a comfortable starting point for shared attention.

Autism

What should I ask school about autism support?

Ask about practical access to learning, communication, sensory comfort, and safety.
