Community guidelines

  • Be kind. Respect identities, disability, family structures, and different ways of communicating.
  • Share experience without diagnosing others or promising cures.
  • Ask before offering advice. No sales pitches, affiliate links, or spam.
  • Keep children’s names, photos, schools, medical records, and identifying details private.
  • Posts and replies are reviewed before other members can see them.

This is peer conversation, not medical care, crisis support, or private messaging. Your nickname and approved messages are visible to people with access to the club.
