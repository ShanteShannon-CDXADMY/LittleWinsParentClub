Potty Training

How do I know my child is ready for potty training?

Watch the skills, not someone else’s timeline.

Little Wins Parenting Companion · Published October 4, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick Answer

The short answer Toilet learning involves body awareness, communication, memory, and planning—not just a birthday.

Jump To The Practical Steps

Use This IdeaResources

The short answerToilet learning involves body awareness, communication, memory, and planning—not just a birthday.

Notice growing awareness

Look for your child recognizing body signals and connecting them with peeing or pooping. Simple, matter-of-fact words help make that connection.

Build the steps together

Your child needs to locate the potty, get there, use it, and communicate when help is needed. Practice the sequence with support rather than expecting instant independence.

Adapt the plan

Your child may be ready for some steps before others. Match expectations to their abilities and ask their clinician about pain, constipation, or concerns.
