What can I try when my baby won’t stop crying?

Check basic needs and make a safe plan for your own break.

Little Wins Parenting Companion · Published October 4, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick Answer

The short answer Check feeding cues, the diaper, temperature comfort, and stimulation. If you feel overwhelmed, put your baby safely down and get support.

Jump To The Practical Steps

Use This IdeaResources

The short answerCheck feeding cues, the diaper, temperature comfort, and stimulation. If you feel overwhelmed, put your baby safely down and get support.

Start with the basics

A diaper change, a feed in response to hunger cues, or quieter surroundings may help. Try gentle rocking or softly singing while holding your baby securely.

A safe pause is allowed

If frustration rises, place your baby on their back in a clear crib or bassinet and take a short break. Call a trusted adult for help. Never shake or hit a baby.

Ask for medical help

Contact your child’s doctor about persistent or unusual crying, feeding problems, poor weight gain, or signs of illness. Do not assume every episode is colic.
