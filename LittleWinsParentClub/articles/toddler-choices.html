How many choices should I give my toddler?

A small choice can make a necessary task easier to begin. The adult still holds the safety limit; the child gets a meaningful say within it.

October 9, 2026 · little wins editorial

Quick Answer

A small choice can make a necessary task easier to begin.

Three Practical Steps

  1. Offer two options you can actually accept.
  2. Keep the sentence short and allow a pause.
  3. Help calmly if choosing is too much.
Use This IdeaResources

Try one small step

  1. Offer two options you can actually accept.
  2. Keep the sentence short and allow a pause.
  3. Help calmly if choosing is too much.

What it might look like

Try “Red cup or blue cup?” rather than asking what they want from an entire cabinet. A child who cannot choose can accept your gentle help.

Make the plan your own

Choose one idea and ask the people involved what support they need. Notice whether it makes the day more manageable, then keep, change, or drop it. You do not have to complete every suggestion. Different ages, abilities, cultures, and family arrangements may call for different choices.
