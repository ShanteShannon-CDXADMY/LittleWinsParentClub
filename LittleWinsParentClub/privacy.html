Privacy & Your Family’s Information

Updated October 9, 2026.

Your planner

Your companion plan—including nicknames, age stages, routines, progress, daily goals, chosen meals, grocery items, and starting preferences—is saved privately in the hosted database under your signed-in site identifier. Existing device plans are moved to that account on first load. We keep device drafts to recover interrupted saves. Shared devices may expose those drafts. Plan versions prevent one device from silently overwriting another device’s changes. The blog companion and standalone companion use separate databases.

Your Favorites

Article, activity, and recipe favorites are stored in this companion app’s hosted database under your signed-in site user identifier, with activity age stages or recipe identifiers and save times. You can remove them from Favorites. The standalone companion app and blog planner use separate databases. Saved legacy journal data can be part of the private companion plan; journal access remains package-gated.

Family Circle Collaboration

We store the circle name, site-specific caregiver identifiers and nicknames, membership roles, shared responsibility titles, assignments, completion state, update times, handover notes, and version numbers in the hosted database. Circle members can view and edit this shared board when live subscriptions are connected; currently Family Circle is a preview. Owners can remove caregivers and revoke unused invitations. Invitation tokens are stored as hashes, expire after seven days, and can be redeemed once. Personal routines and journal entries are separate and are not shared through Family Circle. The standalone companion app and blog planner use separate circles. Email privacy or deletion requests to our business email.

The Parent Club

Posting, replying, thanking, and reporting require ChatGPT sign-in. The community receives a site-specific user identifier. A trusted email is used to recognize the owner’s moderator access. We store your nickname, message, topic, submission time, moderation status, and the user identifiers needed to manage your own posts, reactions, and reports.

Approved nicknames and messages are visible to visitors who can access the site. Pending messages are visible to their author and the moderator. You can remove your own messages from public view; hidden records may remain stored for moderation. Email us to request account-related data deletion. Never post a child’s full name, school, location, photos, or medical records.

Newsletter & Memberships

Current newsletters are web editions. The free weekly digest form saves signup requests and consent. Automatic email sending, paid billing, and verified membership access are not connected. Choosing a package stores a local preference; it does not charge you or enroll your email.

Hosting & Other Websites

The site is hosted through ChatGPT Sites, which processes requests and authentication under its own policies. We have not added advertising pixels or a separate analytics service. External resource and shopping websites have their own privacy policies. Emailing us shares the information in your email with the mailbox provider.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact littlewinsparentclub@gmail.com. Do not include sensitive child information.

Free Weekly Digest Signups

We store your email address, consent time, signup time, subscription status, and a private unsubscribe token in our hosted database. Email delivery is not yet connected. We use these details to manage the free weekly article digest, and do not publish your address. Save the unsubscribe link shown after signup, or contact littlewinsparentclub@gmail.com to request removal. Confirmation and email-footer unsubscribe links must be connected before sending begins.
